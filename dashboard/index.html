Methods note

How this radar is built

One snapshot, fixed coefficients, explicit limits.

Comps value: . The TVL-only OLS fit uses 128 traded-token comparables; category offset is exactly 0. Fitted log market cap is clipped to [0, 14].

Token odds: deterministic logistic regression over all snapshot rows using log TVL, log age, clamped seven-day growth, and large-category indicators. See the scored AUCs and fixed learning recipe below.

Validation: parity of the sum of slug character codes defines A and B; fit A→B and B→A separately. The chronology split trains on listings before 2025-06-01 and tests on later listings. All covariates and labels still come from one sealed snapshot.

Eligibility and exclusions

Wrapped, bridge, and staked products are excluded per the brief. Live-token records are separate from the launch ranking.

    Value of the position

    Farm plans assume 10% of an implied token value is allocated to farmers and a shared $1,000,000 of farmed capital. Per-row expected value is rounded from implied value × 0.10 × deposit / 1,000,000; net subtracts the deposit and estimated gas.

    Gas budget per interaction

    USD assumptions: Ethereum $18; Arbitrum, Optimism, Base $0.40; Polygon and BSC $0.30; Solana $0.15; Avalanche $0.60; other chains $0.75. Each plan budgets four interactions on its first listed chain.

    Deposit and hold

    TVL tiers map to deposits of $50, $100, $150, $200, or $250. Holds are 60 days for age under one year, 45 days under three years, and 30 days otherwise. Plans are templates; verify the live app, contracts, fees, and risks.

    Walk-forward caveat

    The selected model's walk-forward Log R² is −0.421 (MAPE 491.5%). In its 20-row test, rain (listed 2025-10-28; TVL $6.6M, mcap $2.6B) has a +2.93 log residual (~850× fitted); bitway-earn (2026-03-12; $33M, $1.2B) +2.20; and obol (2025-11-04; $1.1B, $717K) −1.87. All remain in the test; no outlier is removed. Jese explicitly relaxed the positivity gate (“ane ga mau tau harus berhasil”); no fit metric changed.
